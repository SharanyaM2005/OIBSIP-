Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India

About

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and the 11th President of India. He played an important role in India's missile and space programs and inspired millions of students through his speeches and books.

Major Achievements

  • Known as the Missile Man of India.
  • 11th President of India (2002–2007).
  • Led India's missile development program.
  • Received Bharat Ratna.
  • Inspired millions of students.
